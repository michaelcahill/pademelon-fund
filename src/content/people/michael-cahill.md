Michael Cahill holds a PhD in Computer Science and has a career spanning IT startups, multinational companies, and academia.

Originally from Gadigal Country, he now works and lives on Muwinina lands, Lutruwita/Tasmania with his wife Rachel, where he also operates the Pademelon Fund, supporting environmental NGOs across Australia.

Michael and Rachel are parents to Patrick, who is studying mathematics in London, and Evelyn, who is studying music in Sydney.
