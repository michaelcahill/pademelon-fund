Rachel Honnery operates the Pademelon Fund while continuing her practice as a multidisciplinary artist examining the fragile intersections between the body, landscape, and ecological systems.

Holding a Master of Fine Arts from UNSW, her work draws on personal experience, material experimentation, and her lived experience with autoimmune disease to explore how human and non-human worlds adapt, deteriorate, and regenerate over time.

Originally from Dharug Country, Rachel now lives and works on Muwinina lands, Lutruwita/Tasmania, where she continues her practice alongside her husband Michael.
