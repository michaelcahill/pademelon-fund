# About Pademelon Fund

Pademelon Fund supports high-impact animal advocacy organisations in Australia.

We believe that by directing resources toward the most effective interventions, we can reduce animal suffering at scale.

## Our Approach

We research and evaluate animal advocacy groups, focusing on those with proven track records of reducing suffering through corporate campaigns, legal advocacy, and public education.
