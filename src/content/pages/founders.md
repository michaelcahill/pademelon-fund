## Rachel Honnery

Rachel Honnery operates the Pademelon Fund while continuing her practice as a multidisciplinary artist examining the fragile intersections between the body, landscape, and ecological systems. Holding a Master of Fine Arts from UNSW, her work draws on personal experience, material experimentation, and her lived experience with autoimmune disease to explore how human and non-human worlds adapt, deteriorate, and regenerate over time. Originally from Dharug Country, Rachel now lives and works on Muwinina lands, Lutruwita/Tasmania, where she continues her practice alongside her husband Michael.

## Michael Cahill

Michael Cahill holds a PhD in Computer Science and has a career spanning IT startups, multinational companies, and academia. Originally from Gadigal Country, he now works and lives on Muwinina lands, Lutruwita/Tasmania with his wife Rachel. He also operates the Pademelon Fund, which supports environmental NGOs across Australia. Michael and Rachel are parents to Patrick, who is studying mathematics in London, and Evelyn, who is studying music in Sydney.
