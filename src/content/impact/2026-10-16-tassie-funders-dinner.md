On Friday 9 October, Pademelon Fund hosted its annual Tassie Funders’ Dinner. It was a great opportunity to meet and share with people, both young and old, in Lutruwita’s/Tassie’s growing environmental movement - lawyers, environmental and First Nations activists, philanthropists and ‘philanthrocrats’ alike. Thank you to all those who came along and contributed to this wonderful evening.
