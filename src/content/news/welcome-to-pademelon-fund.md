# Welcome to Pademelon Fund

We're thrilled to announce the launch of Pademelon Fund, a new initiative dedicated to supporting high-impact animal advocacy organisations in Australia.

## Our Mission

Animal agriculture is one of the leading causes of animal suffering, with billions of animals affected each year. By directing resources toward the most effective interventions — such as corporate campaigns, legal advocacy, and public education — we can reduce suffering at scale.

## What to Expect

In the coming months, we'll be:

- Publishing research on animal advocacy groups in Australia
- Announcing our first grants to high-impact organisations
- Sharing updates on our work and impact

Stay tuned for more updates, and thank you for your support.
